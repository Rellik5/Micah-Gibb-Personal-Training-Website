Built around what you actually do

A pitcher and a forty-year-old who wants their back to stop hurting do not need the same program. What you train, how often, and how hard all come from your sport, your schedule and your history.

Recovery is in the plan, not after it

Sleep, load management and time off are written into the program from the start. Most people do not get hurt because they trained too hard once. They get hurt because nothing was ever scheduled to let them absorb it.

Nutrition you can keep doing

No elimination lists and no weighing every meal. We work on what you eat around training and what you can sustain in a normal week, then adjust from there.

What I work on

Athletic training

Strength and power built for a sport and a season, not for a general fitness goal.

Longevity

Training so you can still do the things you like doing in ten and twenty years. Joints, tendons and capacity, not just numbers.

Nutrition

Fueling around training and recovery, built on habits that survive a busy week.

Recovery

Sleep, load management and return to training after time off or injury.

Results

Every session is logged and reviewed, so progress is something we can point at rather than something you have to take on faith.
