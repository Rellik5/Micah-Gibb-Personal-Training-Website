No crowd, no waiting

One person on the floor at a time. Nobody is watching you, nobody is waiting for the rack, and you are not competing for space with a class that started ten minutes ago.

Everything gets written down

Every set you do is logged. That is how we know whether the plan is working instead of guessing, and it is how you see progress on the weeks when you do not feel it.

The plan bends around your life

Travel, a bad week of sleep, a shoulder that flares up. None of that means starting over. It means the next session looks a little different.

How it works

  1. We talk first

    A free 55-minute session. We go through what you want, what your body currently does, and whether we are a fit. No pressure to sign up.

  2. You get a plan

    Built around your schedule and equipment, not a template. Usually two or three sessions a week to start.

  3. We adjust as you go

    When something stops working we change it, and you will always know why.

The first session is free and costs you an hour.
