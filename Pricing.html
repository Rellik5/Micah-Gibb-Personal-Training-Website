Common questions

Do I have to commit to a number of sessions?

No. Pay session to session for as long as you like. The ten-session block exists because some people prefer it, not because it is required.

What if I need to cancel?

Let me know 24 hours ahead and there is no charge. Inside 24 hours the session is billed, with the obvious exceptions for illness and emergencies.

I have an injury. Can I still train?

Usually yes, once your doctor or physical therapist has cleared you for resistance training. Bring whatever restrictions they gave you and we will work inside them.

Do you write meal plans?

No. I am not a dietitian and writing meal plans would be outside what I am qualified to do. I can talk through general habits and refer you to someone if you need more than that.

What should I bring to the first session?

Shoes you can stand in comfortably and water. That is it. There is no locker room fee and nothing to sign beforehand.
