About

I have been coaching since 2016. Before that I was the person who joined a gym in January and quit by March, three years running.

How I ended up doing this

What changed was working with someone who wrote things down and told me why we were doing them. Within a year I was stronger than I had ever been, and more importantly I understood what I was doing well enough to keep going on my own.

That is the outcome I am after with clients. Not dependence on me, but knowing enough that training becomes something you do rather than something you keep restarting.

Who I work with

Most of my clients have not trained seriously before, or have not trained in years. They are busy, a little skeptical, and tired of programs that assume you already know what a hip hinge is.

I also work with people coming back from an injury, once their physical therapist clears them. If you are training for a specific sport or competition, I am probably not your best option and I will say so on the first call.
